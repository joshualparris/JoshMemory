## Main review conclusion

Josh's strongest apps are the ones with:

1. A real user
2. A real-world job
3. A clear next action
4. A reason to return
5. A clear place inside the broader JoshHub ecosystem

The weaker apps are generally not bad ideas. They are usually:

- Duplicate versions
- Earlier prototypes
- Broken deployments
- Blank or auth-gated pages
- Games with atmosphere but limited replay loop
- Apps with strong structure but not enough content

## Project family review

### JoshHub family

Recommendation:

- Make JoshHub the crown of the ecosystem.
- Do not keep building more overlapping hubs.
- Use JoshHub Apps as the curated public index.
- Use JoshHub Dashboard as the personal daily cockpit.

### Parris Tech family

Recommendation:

- Treat this as the strongest employable/commercial evidence.
- Consolidate or clearly separate Mac and Windows/service variants.
- Add screenshots, README, live link, and a clear portfolio description.

### DCS / Avance / Waypoint family

Recommendation:

- Consolidate into a Professional Growth suite.
- Tie training, work readiness, ICT skill growth, and evidence-of-learning together.

### Family support apps

Recommendation:

- Keep these focused, loving, and simple.
- They are strongest when designed around a real person and a short practical use session.

### Game family

Recommended flagship lanes:

1. Echo Vault for arcade/action
2. Sigil Delve for tactical play
3. Whispering Wilds / Null for text-art adventure

Recommendation:

- Stop creating many tiny fantasy prototypes.
- Finish and curate the best three lanes.

### Lore / research family

Recommendation:

- Keep as a showcase for worldbuilding, lore mapping, and research.
- Do not treat it as the main productivity suite.

### Broken and duplicate apps

Recommendation:

- Create a canonical App Atlas.
- Mark apps as Current, Prototype, Archive, Broken, or Legacy.
- Hide or repair broken links.
- Avoid having five URLs for the same project unless the versions are clearly labelled.

## Recommended missing modules

### 1. Family Care OS

Purpose:

- One place for the family load
- Capacity, routines, appointments, notes, admin, and who is carrying what

Suggested JoshHub location:

- `Family → Care Load`

### 2. Repair & Connection

Purpose:

- A gentle relationship support space, not an argument tracker

Possible features:

- Daily check-in
- Gratitude
- Prayer prompts
- Repair scripts
- Boundary reminders
- Body/emotion awareness
- Shared decisions
- Conflict recovery support

### 3. Work & Income Cockpit

Purpose:

- Calm visibility over employment, availability, job applications, income scenarios, and upskilling

Possible features:

- Work pattern
- Availability
- Applications
- Training goals
- Income modelling
- Evidence of skill growth

### 4. ICT Skill Builder

Purpose:

- Practical IT growth app for base knowledge and workplace execution

Modules could include:

- CompTIA-style fundamentals
- Ticket triage
- Windows troubleshooting
- Microsoft 365 basics
- Printers, Wi-Fi, MFA, DNS, SSL/TLS

Possible features:

- Sleep

- Stress

- Food
- Movement

- Patterns that help with groundedness and regulation

Purpose:

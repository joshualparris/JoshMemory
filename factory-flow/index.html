    THE ROAD TO MASTERY
    DESIGN PRINCIPLE

    Every product must physically travel through the factory. Make something work. Then make it better.

    OPERATIONS MANUAL // REV 01

    Build the line. Own the flow.

    01

    Watch the starter line

    You're already mining and smelting iron. Ore moves along the arrows, enters a furnace, becomes plates and travels to the green shipping dock on the far right. Earn credits by shipping products.

    02

    Build a copper line

    Select a drill (2), click the copper deposit at the lower left, then add a conveyor (1), a smelter (3), and east-facing conveyors towards the lower shipping dock. Rotate a planned building with R. Right-click existing belts or machines to rotate.

    03

    Make complex products

    New machines unlock as contracts finish. Gear presses consume two iron plates. Circuit benches consume one iron and one copper plate from any side. Research labs consume one gear and one circuit. All machines output in the direction of their arrow.

    04

    Manage power and money

    Solar arrays provide 16 power each. Machines draw power while running; add more arrays if demand exceeds supply. Every delivered product earns credits. Clearing a building refunds half the purchase price. Use Z to undo a build, or export a portable save.

    Keyboard: 1–8 select tools · R turn build direction · Delete dismantle · Z undo · Space pause · Esc close.

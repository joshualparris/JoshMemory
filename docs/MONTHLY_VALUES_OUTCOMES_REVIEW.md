# Monthly values and outcomes review

**Purpose:** 10 minutes to decide whether the AI/project ecosystem is making
life more faithful and free, not merely more busy. This is a reflection, not a
product, leaderboard, spiritual score, surveillance system, or new backlog.

## Start with evidence (three minutes)

Review only authorised repositories and existing checkpoints, without cloning
private family, prayer, health or relationship information into this public repo.

- **Verified finished:** up to three real releases/fixes with source, test and
  (where relevant) deployed evidence; otherwise say *unknown*.
- **Actual burden removed:** what is easier, less repetitive or simpler now?
  State evidence; do not assume commits mean improvement.
- **Unfinished / regressed:** name one recurring friction, failure or time cost.
- **Consolidation:** which tool, automation, repo, task or dashboard could be
  stopped, archived, merged or left alone?

## Ask, don't infer (four minutes)

Josh can answer any, all or none. Do not infer relationships, spiritual state,
health or family satisfaction from GitHub activity or other data.

1. Has this work made me more present to God and the people I love?
2. Has it protected sleep, movement, recovery, friendship and service?
3. What has become genuinely easier or more peaceful this month?
4. Is something demanding more attention than the good it provides?
5. What is one small worthwhile next step — or one thing to release?

These are optional conversation prompts. **Do not score, quantify, streak-track,
auto-collect or persist personal answers without explicit, current consent.**

## Finish with a decision (three minutes)

Choose at most **one** of: finish / simplify / pause / delete / continue.
Record a small stop-bounded next action only if useful. If the right decision
is to do nothing, record that as a valid outcome. Check again next month.

speculative character assessments. The monthly review is **read-only by default**:
do not publish, open tasks, change repositories, or notify others without
specific permission.

🏘 Repository Town

How to play

Desktop: WASD or arrows move · drag the mouse to look, or turn on Mouse look (L) so one click captures the mouse and moving it looks around — Esc releases it · Shift runs · C crouches · Space jumps · E uses an exhibit · M toggles the bird's-eye map · Esc closes panels.

Adventure: open the Quest Journal or press J, find glowing relics outside houses, solve their puzzles, and bring all twelve to the Guild Chest near the starting area.

Phone: left thumb drags the joystick · right thumb drags to look · ⤒ jumps · ✋ uses · 🏃 and 🧎 toggle run and crouch.

Walk up to a front door and it opens; keep walking to go inside. Walk back out through the door you came in by. Your position saves automatically.

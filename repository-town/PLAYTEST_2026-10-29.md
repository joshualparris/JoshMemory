# Repository Town browser playtest and fixes — 29 October 2026

Baseline: JoshMemory `main` b7f6a37. Branch `fix/playtest-bugfix-20261029`. Owner: Claude.

## Environment (what this evidence is, and is not)
- Local Playwright 1.56 with the installed headless Chromium (SwiftShader WebGL). **The 3D town rendered** — unlike earlier cloud sessions — but at ~1–2 fps, so timing-sensitive tests can fail under CPU load.
- Played against a local `tools/serve.mjs` build. Not hosted staging/production, not a real Android handset, no listening review. The AI chat endpoint and the external voicebank were unreachable (offline fallbacks exercised instead).

## What was played
- Every HUD button at 1280×800 and 393×852 (phone): open, read, close by Esc and by ✕.
- Walking with real keys; entering/leaving houses A1, O2, W4, F8, M5, C3, B1, D1, J1, L1 on foot; using every exhibit in six houses; upstairs floors.
- Entering all 118 shops/facilities; buying at Bakery, General Store, Greengrocer, Butcher, Hardware (wallet arithmetic checked at the food shops: $75 → $48 for $5+$6+$4+$12; goods appeared in the Bag).
- Address search (case, whitespace, unknown and HTML-like input), Food & rest, Bag, Wallet.
- Driving a summoned car with deterministic physics steps: 50 km/h limiter, collision stop, reverse, park and exit clear of colliders.
- Town Guide and villager chats (offline fallback), NPC Lounge recordings, house tune exhibits, a simulated day (no NaN positions across 171 people).

## Bugs fixed
1. **Esc did not close Settings, Quest Journal, Yarns or Dialogue settings.** Clicking a HUD button left focus on it and `Input` ignored every key from a focused button. Esc now reaches `back` from buttons/links/sliders; text fields keep it.
2. **HUD overlays stacked.** The Quest Journal and Dialogue settings stayed open under later panels. Opening one HUD overlay now dismisses the others; a second press still toggles.
3. **Desktop chats hid "Try asking" and Recorded conversations.** Chat auto-focuses the composer on desktop for any NPC without a scripted conversation, and focus switched on phone "typing mode". Typing mode is now touch-only.
4. **Version · saves badge covered panel text on phones** (same z-index as panels, added later). Moved below panels and the info card.
5. `__town.state().levelName` said "Ground floor" upstairs (test hook only; players saw the right title).
6. `tests/town-recordings-browser.mjs` clicked a Close button the NPC Lounge hides by design; it failed on `main`. Now passes all 20 clips at both widths.

Regression tests: `tests/hud-escape-browser.mjs`, `tests/npc-desktop-prompts-browser.mjs` (both fail on b7f6a37, pass here).

## Not bugs / left alone
- Expansion shops such as The Corner Grocer, pharmacy and library have staff but nothing to buy: tracked as RTBUILD tasks ("interior detail pending"), not touched.
- "Play" exhibits in houses play the house's six-note tune; not arcade games.
- Failures of `tap-walk-browser` (phone), `cafe-play` (desktop / reload), `people.mjs` and `life.mjs` during the parallel run: see results below.

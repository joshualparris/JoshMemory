## Bugs fixed
1. **Esc did not close Settings, Quest Journal, Yarns or Dialogue settings.** Clicking a HUD button left focus on it and `Input` ignored every key from a focused button. Esc now reaches `back` from buttons/links/sliders; text fields keep it.
2. **HUD overlays stacked.** The Quest Journal and Dialogue settings stayed open under later panels. Opening one HUD overlay now dismisses the others; a second press still toggles.
3. **Desktop chats hid "Try asking" and Recorded conversations.** Chat auto-focuses the composer on desktop for any NPC without a scripted conversation, and focus switched on phone "typing mode". Typing mode is now touch-only.
4. **Version · saves badge covered panel text on phones** (same z-index as panels, added later). Moved below panels and the info card.
5. `__town.state().levelName` said "Ground floor" upstairs (test hook only; players saw the right title).
6. `tests/town-recordings-browser.mjs` clicked a Close button the NPC Lounge hides by design; it failed on `main`. Now passes all 20 clips at both widths.

7. **The Corner Grocer (F7-N supermarket) had a cashier but sold nothing.** It now has stock targets, trucks restock it from the same producers as the General Store, its checkout offers Buy buttons, and it joins the staffed-retail rule (no sale without a cashier at the till). Counter trading only; RTBUILD-006's full interior acceptance stays open.
8. **Residents cooked dinner in the wrong room in 11 homes** (Y5, V2, W2, D4, F4, Q4, S4, I6, M6, Q6, E7). A themed exhibit room with a coffee machine outranked the household's real kitchen, so e.g. Y5's Ada spent dinner milling about the hallway (`tests/life.mjs` failed on `main`). Rooms are now ranked stove > fridge/pantry > coffee machine; residents head for the stove room.
9. `tests/cafe-play.mjs` checked tray collection through a status line that another message can replace a frame later; it now checks the ticket state (the game behaved correctly).

Regression tests (each fails on b7f6a37, passes here): `tests/hud-escape-browser.mjs`, `tests/npc-desktop-prompts-browser.mjs`, `tests/supermarket-counter.mjs`, `tests/resident-kitchen.mjs` (all 188 homes). Registered in `test:town` / `test:browser`.

# Narromine workplace interiors: evidence checklist

Goal: every Narromine workplace interior built from evidence about the real business, not a generic fit-out.
Owner request, 6 Nov 2026. Work through the list top to bottom (most-visited places first). For each business:

1. Search the business's own website and social pages, commercial sale/lease listings (often with interior
   photos and floor plans), Narromine Shire Council development applications, news stories about openings or
   renovations, and freely licensed photos (Wikimedia Commons, Mapillary).
2. Record each source with its date in `src/world/narromine-interior-evidence.js`, build only the rooms and
   fittings the evidence supports, and say what is still unknown.
3. Google Maps / Street View photos are **not** used: Google's terms forbid storing or analysing them.
   Josh can compare by eye on the compare page.
4. If nothing usable exists, mark it `no evidence found` with the date searched; it keeps the generic fit-out,
   and the game says so when you walk in.

Status key: `[ ]` not started · `[~]` partly evidenced (descriptions, no photos/plans) · `[x]` built from photos or plans · `[-]` searched, nothing usable

Note: several listing sites (JLL, Gday Pubs) are blocked from the cloud build environment, so their photos could
not be viewed; facts quoted in search results are used and cited.

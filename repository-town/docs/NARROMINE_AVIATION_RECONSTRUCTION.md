### Historical Timeline
- **1929:** **Narromine Aero Club** founded — one of Australia's oldest regional aero clubs, visited in the 1920s–30s by Sir Charles Kingsford Smith, Nancy-Bird Walton, and Jean Batten.
- **June 1940 – July 1944:** **RAAF No. 5 Elementary Flying Training School (No. 5 EFTS)** established under the Empire Air Training Scheme (EATS), operating de Havilland DH.82 Tiger Moths; approximately 2,800–2,850 pilots graduated out of 3,734 trainees.
- **1942 – 1944:** **RAAF No. 8 Operational Training Unit (No. 8 OTU)** also stationed at Narromine Aerodrome.
- **Post-WWII (~25 years):** Served as Qantas's official alternate international airport for Sydney (Mascot) and developed into an internationally renowned thermal soaring and gliding centre.
- **October 2002:** **Narromine Aviation Museum** opened in a volunteer-built complex shared with the Narromine Aero Club and gliding clubs.

## 3. Verified Museum Exhibits & Heritage Monuments (`NARROMINE_AVIATION_EXHIBITS`)

1. **CAC CA-27 Sabre Mk 32 (`A94-915`) Gate Guardian (`[-5710.5, -2068.5]`):**
   - Outdoor Commonwealth Aircraft Corporation Avon-Sabre fighter jet mounted in a climbing attitude on a raked steel pylon and concrete memorial plinth in the museum forecourt.
2. **1903 Wright Flyer Full-Scale Replica (`VH-XWF`) (`[-5722.5, -2053.2]`):**
   - Full-scale airworthy replica of the 1903 Wright Flyer (`12.3 m` wingspan biplane with forward canard elevator and twin rear pusher propellers) built in Narromine for the 2003 centenary of powered flight, displayed inside the walkable Main Exhibition Hangar (`osm:218`).
3. **1930s Corben Super Ace (`VH-UXP`) (`[-5731.5, -2051.5]`):**
   - Single-seat parasol-wing sport aircraft displayed inside `osm:218`.
4. **Rolls-Royce Merlin Mk 25 V12 Aero Engine (`[-5716.2, -2058.2]`):**
   - Preserved WWII liquid-cooled V12 supercharged aero engine mounted on an exhibition cradle inside `osm:218`.
5. **WWII RAAF No. 5 EFTS & No. 8 OTU Memorial Wall (`[-5720.5, -2071.5]`):**
   - Stepped red-brick memorial wall with **33 individual bronze commemorative plaques** honouring the ~2,800 Tiger Moth graduates of No. 5 EFTS (1940–44) and No. 8 OTU.
6. **Narromine Soaring Centre 15 m Standard-Class Sailplane (`[-5628.0, -2034.0]`):**
   - High-aspect-ratio (`15.0 m` wingspan) white fiberglass sailplane with red wingtips and winglets parked on the Gliding Club tie-down apron outside `osm:217`.

# Bracken House and Yeoval UPA: fictional staffing, resident life and housing
**Project:** Repository Town | **Implementation:** 27 October 2026

## Research and boundaries
Research repositories reviewed:
- [BrackenHouse](https://github.com/joshualparris/BrackenHouse): README, `research/nursing/STAFFING-AND-QUALITY-DATA.md`, `research/nursing/NURSING-COVERAGE-AUDIT.md`, historical night/operational research. Bracken reportedly has **72–74 places**, including a **20-bed secure dementia-specific Lavender Wing**. Exact 2026 occupancy, resident identities, beds occupied, staffing establishment, per-shift roster and eMAR remain unverified. This simulator uses a declared **fictional 72-person cohort**, including **20 fictional Lavender-cohort residents**, without mapping their real rooms or secured doors.
- [YeovalAgedCare](https://github.com/joshualparris/YeovalAgedCare): README and `docs/UPA_YEOVAL_BUILDING_DESIGN_AND_GAMEPLAY_RESEARCH_2026-10-26.md`. Public and historical UPA materials reference **25 places**, including single/shared accommodation, lounge/MAC activities, kitchen, meals, connected wings, community health and visiting allied health. Do not mistake 25 registered places for a verified census or ward-by-ward room count. The simulator uses a **fictional 25-person cohort**; Netherwood's six-bed reopening is NOT assumed completed.
- Official Australian Department of Health, Disability and Ageing **2026 24/7 RN guide**: https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/care-minutes-registered-nurses-aged-care/24-7-rns. The **care-minutes guide** gives the sector-wide 215 total/44 RN minutes per resident per day for reference: https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/care-minutes-registered-nurses-aged-care . These are **not verified Bracken or Yeoval individual care-minute requirements**. Rural/remote exemptions, if applicable, are not claimed for these fictional institutions.

## Implemented systems
1. `src/world/aged-care-operations.js`: persistent deterministic *virtual* roster and 97 distinct, entirely invented resident characters. No actual resident-identifying data, diagnoses, medications, clinical notes or exact room assignments.
2. Bracken (72): 79 fictional employees — 10 RN, 7 EN, 30 personal care workers/AINs, plus kitchen/cooks, lifestyle, cleaning, laundry, administration, maintenance, physiotherapy, clinical management and chaplaincy. Core direct-care slots per 24 hours: 7 RN shifts, 5 EN shifts, 21 care-worker shifts, each 8 hours. Simulated scheduled capacity **220 direct-care minutes** and **46.7 RN minutes per simulated resident/day**. These values are scheduling estimates, not evidence of actual hours delivered.
3. Yeoval (25): 39 invented staff members including RN, EN, AIN/personal-care, cooking, lifestyle, cleaning, laundry and reception. Core slots: 3 RN, 2 EN, 7 care-worker shifts daily. **230.4 simulated direct care** and **57.6 RN minutes per resident/day** (small rural home's continuous RN cover raises the per-capita figure). Do NOT claim verified service performance, scope certification or staffing awards.
4. Seven-day three-shift RN presence (07:00–15:00, 15:00–23:00, 23:00–07:00) at **both campuses**, with on-duty roster across midnight. Staff rotation preserves identities, prevents two eight-hour assignments in one calendar day and bounds each role to <= 5 shifts in each seven-day simulated week. Nurses retain their own job types; ENs work with RN oversight and personal-care workers do not independently assume RN responsibilities.
5. `src/world/aged-care-population.js`: visible 3D *representative* residents and on-shift nurses, carers, cooks etc. in both existing interpretive campus interiors. Capped on-screen actors for Android; more residents exist as simulated cohort rather than overlapping 72 meshes at reception. Visible activities change with breakfast, lunch, gardens, rest, evening and overnight. The resident and workforce noticeboard shows the **current** simulated shift and staffing rather than a static decorative employee. Bracken Lavender restrictions and real floor/security layout are intentionally **not simulated or disclosed**.
6. Existing, unoccupied southern town plots are allocated to fictional care workers through `assignCareHousing`. Their interiors gain private sleeping rooms, ordinary shared kitchen, living area, laundry and role-specific details. Staff with no vacant house are represented as commuters, not teleported into occupied homes. Existing 171 town employees and 116 occupied homes are left unchanged. Homes and generic private-room positions are entirely fictional, not addresses for real employees.
7. Facilities use the existing game clock/calendar, so Sunday and overnight care continue. Walking interactions, room model, existing front doors and building geometry remain attached to the pre-existing Bracken/Yeoval campuses; their actual architectural plans remain unverified.

## Critical caveats
- Workload calculations are **theoretical rostered direct-care capacity**: they do not prove minutes *delivered*, quality of care, acuity-adjusted home targets, paid meal breaks, leave cover, sick relief or real-world legal compliance. The simulation is not nursing guidance or a safe clinical staffing tool.
- Real patient records, dementia status, access-control details, security procedures, actual staff movements and private resident identities are never imported.
- These are **fictional character names and approximations** grounded in the scope and capacity of source research. The game is not an as-built ward/safety model.

## Regression and acceptance
- `node tests/aged-care-operations.mjs` — 28 days and three shifts each, coverage, staff identity, no double-booking, care-minute arithmetic, south homes.
- `node tests/aged-care-population.mjs` — both physical interior models, non-overlapping model placement, day/night population and information boards.
- `node tests/aged-care-staff-houses.mjs` — representative actual southside house interiors retain shared facilities and separate staff bedrooms.
- Added to `npm run test:town`, which is part of `npm run build:release`. GitHub Pages publication only after the separate full build, soak tests and browser playtest release gate.

**No real patient or employment facts should be inferred from this game.**

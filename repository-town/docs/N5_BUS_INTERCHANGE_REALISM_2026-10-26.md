# N5-N Repository Community Bus Interchange — modelled regional shelter (RTBUILD-069)
26 October 2026

## Why this scale?
N5-N sits on an existing north-facing road plot, with a **10 m × 7 m** available frontage. A multi-platform coach terminal would be misleading and would overlap neighbouring plots, pedestrian space or roadway. This is now a **small one-stand community interchange**, with a covered passenger waiting area and compact information desk. The existing public site identity, address and fictional town setting are preserved. No real route, operator, live bus tracking, transport approval or legal certification is implied.

## NSW research applied in-game
- [TfNSW Functional Spaces Part 4, TS 04951.4, 2 Dec 2024](https://standards.transport.nsw.gov.au/search-standard-specific/?id=AST+-+0004461%3A2022): a defined bus boarding hardstand, clear access to front and rear bus doors, accessible manoeuvring, 150 mm kerb where provided, seating and weather protection. The rendering shows the 150 mm kerb edge as a **visual** treatment only, so the player's walk remains at ground level.
- [TfNSW Bus Stop Urban Design Guideline, 2025](https://www.transport.nsw.gov.au/system/files/media/documents/2025/bus-stop-urban-design-guideline.pdf): separate passenger boarding, waiting, verge and road zones; a visible stop sign, timetable, shade, seating and lighting.
- [Australian Human Rights Commission bus stop guidance](https://humanrights.gov.au/resource-hub/by-resource-type/publications/guidelines/guideline-compliance-bus-stops-accessible-public-transport): at least 1200 mm clear access path; reference manoeuvring envelope 2070 × 1540 mm for 180° turn; tactile indicators and wheelchair ramp deployment space not blocked by seats/advertising.
- [Australian Government Whole Journey Guide, §3.3](https://www.infrastructure.gov.au/infrastructure-transport-vehicles/transport-strategy-policy/disabilities/whole-journey/guide/3-3-stop-station): comfort, clear wayfinding, and consideration of seating, drinking water, toilets, accessible information and service updates.

## Built into game
- Metal rain/sun canopy with glazed side/rear panels, open front portal matching internal return-to-street doorway, awning downlights and low-reflection clear viewing panels.
- Paved forecourt, one clearly marked bus pole, yellow visual warning and directional tactile indicators, separate hardstand and low front-edge kerb. **No bus animation, functioning ramp or actual service timetable is fabricated.**
- Interior waiting area has two marked priority bench positions, information desk with visible monitor, route map/timetable, emergency guidance, bin and bottle-water point. Displays are explicitly fictional.
- Sheltered waiting area is **public 24 hours**; the staffed information role retains daytime working hours. This is not a locked shop or a claimed staffed 24-hour office.
- One main door faces **-Z**, towards the existing road; interior and external sensor share the same entry axis. Existing safety escape and in-game public facilities remain unchanged.
- Tests exercise geometry, accessible walking paths, all interaction points and front-door exit on desktop WebGL.

## Deliberate limitations
This is a low-poly walkable **simulation**, not a construction drawing or an NCC/DSAPT compliance certificate. Real delivery still requires bus operator/road authority consultation, swept-path and road safety review, kerb/grade survey, AS 1428 assessment, tactile spacing, acoustic/light measurements, electrical design, accessibility review and emergency/access management. Routes, arrival times and head-of-stop location on a real road cannot be asserted without operator/GIS data. The place remains fictional.

# Repository Town: origins and complete illustrated-history basis, before settlement–2026

**Canonical date:** 25 October 2026. **This is historical fiction anchored in verifiable central-west NSW geographic and engineering context.** The fictional town is **not a real gazetted NSW settlement**. We are defining an alternate local history for the game's 234-site grid, not claiming an unrecorded mayor, knighthood, Aboriginal history or railway siding in actual NSW archives.

**Map anchor:** **-32.273600, 148.408400** (WGS84 decimal degrees), west of the existing village of Minore. [Open the chosen ground on OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=-32.273600&mlon=148.408400#map=15/-32.273600/148.408400). This is a precisely specified *game-centre coordinate* in a rural district, **not proven vacant, owned by the project, zoned for a township, survey-approved, flood-free or connected to utilities**. The schematic project footprint is 780 m east–west × 480 m north–south (37.44 ha) using game metres; land lot boundaries and safe buildable area **have not been verified**.

## The origin story

Before there was a railway halt, a pub or a surveyor's rectangular grid, the land around the Wambuul–Macquarie belonged to ongoing Aboriginal cultural landscapes and nations. The river bears an officially recognised dual name, **Macquarie River / Wambuul**. Different sources describe Wiradjuri, Wailwan, Ngemba and associated cultural connections across the wider basin. **Do not invent an Aboriginal Elder, a pre-contact place-name or an Indigenous consent process for the chosen point.** Traditional Owners must be consulted before treating any real site as developable. Sources S04–S05.

With Dubbo connected to rail in **1881**, and the line extending to Narromine/Nevertire in **1882**, farms west of Dubbo gained a realistic reason to develop a wayside cluster. In the fictional Repository timeline, **Reginald Arthur Repo (born 1856)** and co-founder **Ellen Repo** choose a cart-track location near the corridor and build **Repo's Stores in 1883**—a timber slab store, cart shed and cramped overnight dwelling at the future **G8-N Town Hall** site. This is the town's **first permanent colonial-era building**, *not* the first human-made building on the land. There is no known physical record because Repository Town is fictional.

Over the next decade, Repo's shop becomes a trading post, water-cart station, smithy's stop and schoolroom. Farm families call the junction **Repo's Cross Roads**, and railway workers shorten an informal goods-loading petition to **Repo's Halt**. As more businesses arrive, the name becomes **Repository**—a pun on the original surname that also reflects the storehouse and, in the game's 2026 setting, the town's digital repositories.

The **first mayor**, in the fictional local council story, is **Reginald Arthur Repo**, serving **1909–1914**. He is *not* a real registered NSW councillor. The later title **Sir Reginald Repo** comes from an expressly fictional **1927 honour**; he was **Reginald Repo, not yet Sir**, during his first mayoral term. He dies in the fictional chronology in **1940**. The existing playable mayor **Vern** remains mayor in the game's current 2026 era, with Arlo and Sadie as rivals; do **not** spawn Reginald as a modern NPC.

The first store is dismantled in **1910** and its timber recycled into the town hall. The **Branch Line Hotel** is constructed in **1911** and opens **1912**, later refitted in **1958**, refrigerated in **1988** and renamed **The Merged Branch** in **2019**. These dates are **already canonical** in the pub's separate history. The nearby **Railway Hotel at I8-S** is a separate establishment with a different function and must never be merged into the pub's history. No actual early hotel licence is invented.

## Physical setting and consequences

The town is fictional but the wider **Minore–Narromine–Dubbo** area is real: Minore is about **4.7 km**, Narromine **16.5 km** and Dubbo **18.8 km** from the chosen map anchor as **straight-line centre-point distances, not driving distances**. Existing westbound travel uses the **Mitchell Highway corridor**; the Main Western railway passes through the district. The **fictional local access spur** and game railway siding are not actual surveyed intersections, level crossings or service timetables.

The game grid's x-axis increases **east**; its z-axis increases **south**. The current eight existing street road rows and the separately designated north/south addresses remain unchanged. **There is no added street 9 or street 11**; row-9-designated commercial frontage is reached from the already modelled road 8. The historic G8-N plot remains the current Town Hall and N8-S the pub. Existing 188 houses + 46 non-house premises stay at their existing grid IDs.

**This is not an application to occupy the ground.** The exact candidate location has not yet passed real land ownership, cadastral, planning, AHIMS, biodiversity, topography, flood hazard, groundwater quality, transport access or network capacity checks. A game can overlay a fictional village for research without asserting any right to build there.

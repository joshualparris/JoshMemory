# Repository Town 2026 — all missing buildings, staged exterior-first

**Implementation scope:** 72 newly registered, street-addressed sites in pre-existing vacant **north-facing plots**; 22 Priority-1 and 50 Priority-2. Existing **46** business/civic sites and **188** houses are preserved. The town uses its established Minore alternative-universe planning designation. **No new roads** and no use of row-8 railway, row-9 protected farm fields or row-10/11 planning bands.

**Priority order:** the nine free-standing first-wave sites (library, pharmacy, fuel stop, RepoTech, nursery, barber, office/print, public amenities/playground, childcare), then other Priority 1, then Priority 2. **Tenth first-wave project: painters** is an expansion of existing W9-N Town Trades & Maintenance; it is not a duplicate new lot. Existing hardware at V9-N remains. Actual fully modelled interiors, equipment, operating permits, utility hookups and building-code acceptance remain **future individual-building tasks**.

**Important spatial honesty:** northern frontages face a street across from existing house fronts. The candidate plot namespace was previously labelled 'not surveyed'; being empty in address registry is *not proof of clearance of existing house backyards, fences or vegetation*. Automated geometry and interactive playtests must establish no collision. Large facilities get compact **gatehouse/pavilion/office** at a single address and do *not* purport to fit full ovals, cemeteries, showgrounds or holiday parks within the 26 m frontage.

## Follow-up specialist work

Every venue needs room-by-room measured plans, accessible door and circulation, structural/fire design, roof/service penetrations, live utility connections with outages, trade-specific operating requirements, equipment, consumables, workers and inventories. Public toilets need separated cubicles, drainage, basins and accessibility; the petrol station needs realistic site/fire/pollution controls; childcare needs outdoor space and child-safe circulation; nursery needs outdoor shade, live stock and water demand. Service stations, caravan parks and sports grounds must have honest land footprints and realistic road access. With the current small lot-depth assumptions, oversized facilities may have to **relocate to a separately surveyed alternative-universe reserve** while retaining their working booking/visitor office on these plots.

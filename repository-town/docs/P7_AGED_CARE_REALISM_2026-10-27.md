# RTBUILD-016 — P7-N Seniors & Aged-Care Services realism brief

Prepared 27 October 2026. **Fictional small-household demonstration, not a surveyed or certified UPA building or full aged-care home.**

## Starting point and limits

The original P7-N agedcare service used a generic health-office facade and a generic shared one-room reception fallback. The existing Repo Town northern plots have a test-protected maximum footprint of 13m x 11m to protect adjacent lots. A full residential home cannot honestly fit there. The new build is an illustrative, one-storey **13 x 11m, two-bedroom household annex**, with the game's unchanged road-facing front at local Z=7 and rear at Z=-4.

## Authoritative sources

1. Australian Government National Aged Care Design Principles and Guidelines (July 2024): enable the person, cultivate a home, access the outdoors, connect with community. Domestic surroundings, privacy, dignity, accessible corridors, enabling design and safe care workplaces are key. https://www.health.gov.au/resources/publications/national-aged-care-design-principles-and-guidelines
2. UPA Yeoval official listing: homelike country atmosphere, single/double rooms with ensuites and emergency call, communal dining, round-the-clock care and dementia-specific support. Its exact layout is NOT published by this source. https://www.upa.org.au/locations/dubbo/yeoval-aged-care/
3. Bracken House Dubbo's My Aged Care listing: much larger site, private ensuite accommodation, communal dining, activities, secure dementia care garden and a chapel. Advertised 22-26 m2 rooms are reference dimensions, not evidence of this fictional annex's approval. https://www.myagedcare.gov.au/find-a-provider/search/5jt9znlp-united-protestant-association-of-nsw-limited/aged-care-homes/bracken-house-dubbo-nkm7wg
4. NSW Seniors Housing Design Guide section 7.2: homelike character, smaller households, verandahs, accessible outdoor connection and places to rest. https://www.planning.nsw.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-12/seniors-housing-design-guide.pdf
5. Aged Care Quality and Safety Commission dementia-friendly design guidance: https://www.agedcarequality.gov.au/quality-standards/designing-dementia-friendly-care-environments

## Implemented building

Exterior: low warm-brick/cream building, green roof, shaded porch, glazed central entrance, extensive residential windows, garden planters, bench, step-free-looking entry surface and warm entry lights. This is a fictional regional NSW building; no real UPA image, trademark or unsupported surveyed floor plan is reproduced.

Interior: entrance/exit, reception and staff handover, domestic living/activity lounge and registered seats, shared dining and illustrative kitchenette, central circulation, two private bedrooms with beds, linen, call buttons and ensuite alcoves, accessible-looking staff storage illustrations, warm lighting, side windows and six interactive information points. Colliders follow beds, cupboards and partitions; door gaps are real, rather than non-functional graphics.

The project uses a deliberately tiny model: it CANNOT reasonably provide full-scale separate clean/dirty utility rooms, kitchen, laundry, multiple independent fire exits, appropriate hoist turning circles, resident garden loop, car parking, and complete staff facilities on this lot. Cabinets and an outdoor-design exhibit DO NOT prove those features exist or comply. A genuinely full-scale residential-care home requires a larger plot and dedicated game expansion.

## Validation and remaining work

The facade and interior should both measure 13 x 11m, keep the P7-N front street-facing exit, permit entry to both bedrooms without clipping, provide a clear central path, and allow all visitor hotspots to be reached. See focused regression tests in tests/aged-care.mjs.

Outstanding independent verification: genuine hosted browser walk/enter/exit/phone test, full privacy and release gate, and live deployed fingerprint. Real building approvals would separately require design review against current Aged Care Act/Quality Standards, NCC, fire safety, accessibility/AS 1428 and NSW planning by qualified people. Do not present game geometry as legal certification.

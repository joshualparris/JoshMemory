## Other occupations
- [ ] Bakery: weigh ingredients, mix, shape, proof, bake, cool and pack real production batches.
- [ ] Delivery driver: accept manifest, load correct parcels, drive route, unload and confirm receipt; businesses receive usable stock.
- [ ] Cleaner: collect tools, pick up rubbish, empty bins, mop spills; visible cleanliness and safer surfaces.
- [ ] Farmhand: water/weeding, animal feed, eggs, harvest and storage; production enters supply chain.
- [ ] Trades: diagnose faults, select tools/parts, isolate equipment, repair and test; real lights/taps/machines restored.
- [ ] Post/depot: destination sorting, weighing/labelling, loading, safe stacking and route preparation.

## Work and wages
- [ ] Paid shifts accrue time with reasonable duties and breaks; transparent timesheets.
- [ ] Contracts have scope, agreed price, expenses, acceptance and payment.
- [ ] Skills, training/qualifications, supervised apprenticeships and responsibility unlock progression.
- [ ] Employer finances determine hiring and payment; record unpaid wages as debt rather than erase work.
- [ ] Payslips show hours, rates, gross pay, PAYG and take-home pay, in fictional Town Dollars.
- [ ] Fair error/rework feedback without arbitrary wage deductions.
- [ ] Save/restore all work, avoid duplicated goods/payments, verify desktop/mobile and long-running simulation.

## Experience standard
Work changes something visible; another resident benefits; payment funds a meaningful town life.

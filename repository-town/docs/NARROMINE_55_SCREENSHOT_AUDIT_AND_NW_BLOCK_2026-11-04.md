# Narromine 55-Screenshot Street View Comparison Audit & NW Dandaloo Block Reconstruction (2026-11-04)

## 1. Overview & Provenance Compliance

This document records:
1. A frame-by-frame visual QA audit of all **55 side-by-side comparison screenshots** (`1Screenshot_20261104_152207.png` through `55Screenshot_20261104_153945.png`) captured in `narromine-compare.html` along **Derribong Avenue** and **Dandaloo Street, Narromine NSW 2821**.
2. The architectural reconstruction specification and verification evidence for the **North-West Dandaloo Street Block (`38–68 Dandaloo Street`)** implemented in [`src/world/narromine-nw-dandaloo-evidence.js`](../src/world/narromine-nw-dandaloo-evidence.js) and [`src/world/narromine-nw-dandaloo-block.js`](../src/world/narromine-nw-dandaloo-block.js).

### Strict Provenance & Licensing Rule
- **Google Street View comparison screenshots (`#01`–`#55`)**: Used **strictly for visual QA diagnosis** (identifying missing buildings, height discrepancies, awning/roof form errors, and colour/signage mismatches). Zero pixels, textures, or traced assets from Google Street View are included in the repository.
- **Footprints & Placement**: Derived from surveyed **OpenStreetMap (`ODbL 1.0`)** building polygons (`osm:179`, `osm:180`, `osm:183`, `osm:184`, `osm:185`) and commercial lot frontages (`38–44 Dandaloo Street`) in [`src/world/narromine-osm.json`](../src/world/narromine-osm.json) and [`src/world/narromine-layout.js`](../src/world/narromine-layout.js).
- **Building Elevations**: Grounded in **2023 NSW Spatial Services LiDAR DSM/DEM (`CC BY 4.0`)** eave, ridge, and median height measurements recorded in [`data/narromine-building-inventory.json`](../data/narromine-building-inventory.json).
- **Textures & Geometry**: 100% procedural Three.js geometry and canvas signage generated at runtime.

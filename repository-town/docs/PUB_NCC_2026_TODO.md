## Acceptance and interdisciplinary review gates

1. Pub venue scenario declares current NSW NCC baseline, provisional Class 6, actual intended use, floor/storey layout, approved occupancy and separately tagged *synthetic* engineering assumptions. Source references are dated; exact section/standard version validated before claiming a solution meets any performance requirement.
2. Every modelled public and worker WC, accessible route, door, exit, room, food prep area, sink and appliance has a stable ID, actual coordinates/dimensions and a task-backed game interaction. Merely writing explanatory text does not close an implementation task.
3. Fire, potable water, kitchen sewage/trade waste, electrical loads, possible gas/LPG, lighting and stormwater connect by separate conserved/protected networks, with unrelated outages remaining unaffected and no phantom flows.
4. Inspection of the **actual** deployed pub includes mouse/keyboard and Android controls, entry/exit, kitchen operations, toilet use, step-free navigation, blackout and fire drill as *fictional gameplay*, pest/cleaning/food-safety states, lawful signage and no trap-through-wall bug.
5. Town unit/integration tests, source fingerprint, screenshots, build/CI and actual production URL/version must all be attached before gameplay completion. Even then legal compliance is **not certified**: a suitably qualified certifier, access consultant, fire engineer and licensed trade designers would be necessary for real building design.

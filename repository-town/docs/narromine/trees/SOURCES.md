### Additional botanical morphology/taxonomic sources (not site observations)

- [NSW PlantNET `Brachychiton populneus`](https://plantnet.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/cgi-bin/NSWfl.pl?lvl=sp&name=Brachychiton~populneus&page=nswfl): native evergreen Kurrajong, leaves entire or lobed; up to ~20 m.
- [Botanic Gardens of South Australia, `Pyrus ussuriensis`](https://plantselector.botanicgardens.sa.gov.au/Plants/Details/1056): deciduous Manchurian Pear, broad-conical, typical 9–12 m height and 6–7 m spread, white spring flowers/autumn colour. **Mature typical dimensions are not measured Narromine 2018 plantings**.
- [NSW PlantNET `Eucalyptus camaldulensis`](https://plantnet.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/cgi-bin/NSWfl.pl?lvl=sp&name=Eucalyptus~camaldulensis&page=nswfl): riverine habitat and bark/leaf characteristics.
- [NSW BioNet Inland Riverine Forests vegetation class](https://threatenedspecies.bionet.nsw.gov.au/VegClass?vegClassName=Inland+Riverine+Forests): indicative `E. camaldulensis`, occasional other box eucalypts; broad habitat, not a local plot.
- [HortFlora Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria, `Pyrus ussuriensis`](https://hortflora.rbg.vic.gov.au/taxon/ad95a41e-5340-11e7-b82b-005056b0018f): distinguishes the ornamental tree from `P. calleryana`.
- [PlantNET FloraOnline](https://plantnet.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/) species lookups before importing Latin names.

# Narromine Shire Council — informal tree inventory/GIS records enquiry

**Status:** Ready-to-send draft. **Not sent.** Council customer service: `mail@narromine.nsw.gov.au`; `02 6889 9999`. [Council policies and contact](https://www.narromine.nsw.gov.au/council/policies).

**Suggested subject:** Request for Narromine township tree inventory / GIS tree species asset data

Hello Narromine Shire Council,

I'm mapping Narromine township for a non-commercial/local-history-style 3D digital reconstruction, and I'm trying to represent the actual trees as accurately as possible.

I have found Council's tree-management and nature strip policies, the historic Dandaloo Street planting/design references, the Kurrajong tree reports for Narromine Cemetery, and public NSW vegetation data. I'd like to check **whether Council maintains an individual street/park tree register, planting database, arborist inspection inventory or GIS tree point layer** for Narromine town, and whether a digital extract is available for research.

If a tree register exists, could you please advise whether it can include, where available:

- A unique tree ID or asset reference and **individual location** (latitude/longitude, surveyed GPS, or GIS geometry/CRS);
- Common and scientific species name (including cultivar where recorded);
- Street, park, cemetery, airport or other Council-managed site, and current/historic status;
- Recorded tree height, canopy width, DBH/trunk size and any condition/age estimate;
- Planting, inspection, maintenance, removal or replacement dates;
- The date and accuracy/source of the tree's geolocation;
- Linkage to historic planting/landscape plans or dated photos.

An extract in **GeoJSON, GeoPackage, shapefile or CSV** would be ideal; a current street-tree PDF map or spreadsheet would also help. Please let me know if sensitive fields need to be excluded, what attribution/permissions would apply and whether there are fees.

I would especially appreciate information for:

1. **Dandaloo Street**: any **as-built 2018 Main Street Enhancement Program** Manchurian Pear planting list, cultivar, exact tree count, and subsequent replacement/removal records, alongside the Moir 2019 Dandaloo Street South plan.
2. **Narromine Cemetery**: tree positions and the current status of the **35 Kurrajongs** counted in Council's April 2024 report (20 western avenue and 15 eastern avenue), plus northern boundary Manchurian Pears and any 2025–26 replacement work.
3. **Narromine Aerodrome**: any tree surveys, especially if the 2022 ecological assessment's 7 Poplar Box and 3 Kurrajong can be tied to specific measured positions.
4. **Parks/reserves/riparian corridors**: any street-tree inspection asset data, wetlands planting schedule or High Park River Reserve revegetation plans.

Council's asset-management documents mention **Intramaps and QGIS**; could you confirm whether tree inspection/asset information is maintained in either system or in a separate contractor database?

If Council does not maintain such a dataset, could you point me toward the relevant manager, arborist contractor or public record? I'm happy to start with Dandaloo Street and the cemetery as a small pilot rather than the entire township.

Thank you for your help.

Kind regards

## Request handling guidance

This is an **informal information request**, not a GIPA application. Do not automatically submit a formal application or represent Council as agreeing. If Council replies that an inventory exists but is not routinely public, ask its Records/Information Access team about the NSW Government Information (Public Access) Act informal release or application route.

**Desired reply types:** (1) register exists/does not exist; (2) responsible officer; (3) scope/coverage and last survey; (4) records available as downloadable GIS versus documents; (5) extraction/attribution/conditions. Preserve any reply as dated correspondence, separate from the tree evidence itself.

**If no register:** ask whether maintenance contractors have an arborist assessment with tree ID/species, and whether the 2019 Dandaloo South drawings have CAD/as-built updates. Avoid resurveying publicly managed trees without permission to enter restricted operational areas.

Throughput

Mine ore, run conveyor belts, smelt plates and assemble gears, wire, circuits and motors. Every milestone asks for more, and the last one asks for a steady rate. Find the bottleneck and fix it.

  • Pick a building from the tool rail (keys 1 to 5), press R to rotate it, then click to place. Its arrow is where it pushes items out.
  • Drag with the Belt tool to lay a whole line of conveyor at once.
  • Right-click or use Delete (X) to remove. Drag the empty floor to look around; scroll or use + and − to zoom.
  • Click a building with the Select tool (Q) to see what it is waiting for, and to choose an assembler's recipe.

Your factory saves itself in this browser.

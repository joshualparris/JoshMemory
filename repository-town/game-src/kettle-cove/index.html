Light the lighthouse before midnight.

A dialogue RPG in one repeating day

Kettle Cove

The lighthouse has been dark for a week, its keeper is missing, and every night at midnight the fog rolls in and the day starts over. Talk to the people of the cove, learn what they are hiding, and get that lamp lit before twelve.

  • Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, or tap where you want to go.
  • Talk with E or Space beside someone, or tap them. Pick replies with 1 to 9.
  • Asking about something new takes one bell. A day has eight.
  • Your notebook, skills and experience carry over to the next day. Nothing else does.

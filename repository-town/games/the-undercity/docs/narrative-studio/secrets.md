## Reveal schedule
- **Depth 1:** common rumours, Act I or later; always mark hearsay as such.
- **Depth 2:** late Act I, after a witness or object.
- **Depth 3:** Act II, after independent evidence and a consequence.
- **Depth 4:** Act III, locked to a relevant personal ambition and explicit player choice.
- **Depth 5:** ambitions' finales only; never a random card spoiler.
- Competing accounts remain tagged as rumours until the lead writer resolves the conflict in the bible and dependent storylets.

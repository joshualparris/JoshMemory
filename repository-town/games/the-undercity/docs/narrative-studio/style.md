# Undercity — Style Guide

## Sentence rhythm
Use long, material descriptions for the mechanisms of the city; short sentences for the cost of discovering them.
- “The clerk checks the ledger against the soot on your sleeve, then copies a name from the margin that is not yours.” / “He puts the pen down.”
- “A ferry passes beneath the quays carrying passengers whose tickets all expired in the same forgotten year.” / “Nobody disembarks.”
- “The apprentice measures the lantern twice, once for the Council and once for the street that pays for it.” / “The figures disagree.”

## Imagery
Preferred: brass, chalk, weak lamplight, damp rope, ash, cracked glass, municipal forms, moss and hard stone. Surface sunlight is rare and consequential. Avoid generic forests, tropical landscapes and anonymous shadow-cloud descriptions.

## Dialogue and NPC voices
The Ledger is exact but evasive; Wardens speak of duty; the Hands are practical; Choir members find precision through art; Dawn negotiators distrust power. Give every recurring NPC an immediate want and a durable relationship to their past. No tags like “she said angrily”.

## Humour
Understated civic absurdity without puns:
- “The missing street has been assessed for rates.”
- “The stamp is genuine. The office never existed.”
- “Your witnesses are welcome, provided they arrive separately.”
- “The committee has concluded that the river is being unreasonable.”

## Second-person rule
Describe what the player sees, does and risks. Do not dictate their feelings or praise their morals. Avoid “You are the only person brave enough to…” unless it is explicitly framed as an NPC's potentially unreliable opinion.

## Ten forbidden constructions
“Little did you know”; “A chill runs down your spine”; “Everything changes forever”; “Suddenly, without warning”; “There is more than meets the eye”; “Time will tell”; “You cannot help but”; “A dark secret”; “A glimmer of hope”; “And then you wake up”.

## Three-pass test
**Content:** clarity, grammar, rhythm, voice, copyright and original imagery.
**Design + continuity:** competing choices, cost, secret depth and backwards-compatible outcomes.
**Mechanical:** reachable gates, 2–5 branches for *new* content, declared IDs, earned items, difficulty/CP, menace, zero-action exceptions and no dead-end achievements.

An AI copy editor cannot also certify its own mechanics; independent reviewers and explicit human sign-off are required.

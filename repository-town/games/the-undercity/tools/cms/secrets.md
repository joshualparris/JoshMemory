## Reveal Schedule

- **Depth 1–2:** Acts I–II. Rumours, observations and testimony may appear if clearly attributed.
- **Depth 3:** Act III onward; requires a corroborating piece of evidence or witness.
- **Depth 4:** Acts IV–V only, and only following the named chain gate.
- **Depth 5:** Endgame verdict or epilogue; never reveal in introductory cards or ordinary repeatable encounters.

A continuity agent may flag contradictions; only a human reviewer authorises revisions or deeper disclosure. The client-side editor's depth check is a guardrail, not proof that story prose conceals every secret.

How to play

Desktop: WASD or arrows move · drag the mouse to look (double-click locks the pointer) · Shift runs · C crouches · Space jumps · E uses an exhibit · M toggles the bird's-eye map · Esc closes panels.

Phone: left thumb drags the joystick · right thumb drags to look · ⤒ jumps · ✋ uses · 🏃 and 🧎 toggle run and crouch.

Walk up to a front door and it opens; keep walking to go inside. Walk back out through the door you came in by. Your position saves automatically.

## Distinction between statutory requirements and design guidance

- LEP zoning and legally applicable development controls: statutory context, but site-specific mapping and permissibility **not checked**.
- DCP: local development assessment guidance; numerical controls and applicable chapter **not checked in full**.
- Council standard drawings and engineering standards: design guidance/requirements as adopted in approval conditions; latest drawings and requested standards **not obtained**.
- Austroads: technical road design guidance, not an independent consent or certification.
- AS 2890 and AS 1428: standards with application depending on legal adoption and project context; full licensed text **not accessed**, so no fabricated clause dimensions.
- Repo Town: fictional 3D world near Minore; real NSW approval and actual land title do not follow from game coordinates.
